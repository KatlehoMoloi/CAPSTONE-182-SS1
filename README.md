# Motivation
StadioChoice is an a challenging transitional phase where they are experiencing less reliability on profits coming from a declining satelite customer base to moving towards building a streaming service. StadioStream is still in the early stages of growth and not self sufficient yet. It is a challenging time in the market for such a transition as high valued international investors in the streaming fields are also compete for the same subscribers. Putting a free trial period helps in having an entry point to attracting new streaming customers. The concern is that the percentage of the trial using customers who end up paying subscribers has dropped from 41% to 33% in the last two years. The business also faced an increase in the cost of aquairing a new streaming subscriber, the cost increased from R240 to R355. Hvaing that inverted relationship of decreased converted trial .customer and increased aquiring cost per customer results to a growing financial loss and adding preassure on the streaming division while already operating in a loss
