# Motivation
StadioChoice is an a challenging transitional phase where they are experiencing less reliability on profits coming from a declining satelite customer base to moving towards building a streaming service. StadioStream is still in the early stages of growth and not self sufficient yet. It is a challenging time in the market for such a transition as high valued international investors in the streaming fields are also compete for the same subscribers. Putting a free trial period helps in having an entry point to attracting new streaming customers. The concern is that the percentage of the trial using customers who end up paying subscribers has dropped from 41% to 33% in the last two years. The business also faced an increase in the cost of aquairing a new streaming subscriber, the cost increased from R240 to R355. Having that inverted relationship of decreased converted trial .customer and increased aquiring cost per customer results to a growing financial loss and add pressure on the streaming division while already operating in a loss. While customers are on the trial period, STADIOchoice would collect the behavioural data per household such as the plays, pausing, searches and actions abandent. The data colected in that period is not being used as predictive indicator tool for identifying the customers who are likely to convert and which would be cancelling. If the business puts a predictive model for the trial customer behaviour in place, they would be able to identify customer outcome earlier and which allow an early intervention. Early trial customer intervention could be high customer engagement to fit customer needs, or better onboarding process when trial period ends. This initiative would be beneficial to the business's stratigic focus of being ahead of the streaming market through rentention and not only high sign ups. This will be an initative will show a proactive trial to subscription conversion process.
# Problem Statement
Even though there is a clear fall in trial to paid conversion rate while the cost per streaming subscriber aquired is rising, there is still uncertainty on whther the trial period customer behaviour can be a determinng factor whether trial subscribers are likely to convert to paying customers. Therefore, the aim in my report is to determine whether classification techniqures are reliable enough to accurately predicit trial to paid conversion using the STADIOChoice steaming viewing logs and the subcription and the customer billing information. Using this as evidence will assist in having proactive interventions during the trial period.
